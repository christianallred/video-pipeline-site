Privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Video Pipeline is a personal, self-hosted app. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It has no other users and no servers of its own.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, Google asks for permission to:

  • upload videos and thumbnails to that channel (YouTube upload access), and
  • read the channel's basic details (YouTube read-only access), used only to show which channel is connected.

The app does not read, change or delete other videos, comments, playlists, subscribers, analytics or any other account data.

How the data is used and stored

  • The sign-in tokens Google returns are stored only in a private folder on the owner's computer. They are never uploaded anywhere else, never shared, and never sold.
  • They are used only to upload the owner's finished videos, with the title, description, tags, thumbnail and schedule the owner approved, and to read the connected channel's name.
  • The app collects no analytics, uses no advertising, and does not use cookies other than the owner's own sign-in to the app.
  • No data obtained from Google is used to train AI models or transferred to third parties.

Video Pipeline's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

Video Pipeline uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Pressing Disconnect in the app's Settings deletes the stored tokens and asks Google to revoke them. Access can also be removed at any time from your Google account's third-party access page.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
